Short Bio

Prof. Weijiang Yu is an Associate Professor at School of Computer Science and Engineering in Sun Yat-sen University, Guangzhou, China. Before that, he was a researcher scientist at Huawei with the honor of Huawei Topminds and was a research intern at MSRA, Petuum, SenseTime, and Tencent.

Weijiang received his Ph.D. (2017-2022) from SYSU, advised by Prof. Nong Xiao， B.S. from SCNU. He is fortunate to be a visiting scholar at KAUST and CMU, where he collaborated with Prof. Bernard Ghanem and Prof. Eric P. Xing, respectively. His research interests include Multimodal AI (e.g., Large World Model, SFT, post-training), HPC+AI for Science(e.g., Large model for healthcare, drug discovery), and Machine Learning. He has published many papers in top conferences and journals, such as TPAMI, Nature Communications, NeurIPS, CVPR, ACL and so on.
